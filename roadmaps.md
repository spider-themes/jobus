Jobs are stored in their own dedicated WordPress database system, keeping them separate and organized away from your regular blog posts.

### Employer Profile Management
Employers get their own dedicated profiles where they can showcase their company history, location, and workplace culture to candidates.

## 🔴 Rejected

### Legacy Architecture Terminology
Outdated plugin naming structures were completely replaced for a clean transition to the modern framework.

### Legacy Manual Mail Protocols
Outdated manual email systems were stripped out in favor of faster, automated email delivery routing.

### Obsolete Database Meta Tracking
Legacy tracking data that slowed down the system was purged to improve active lookup speeds dramatically.

### Monolithic Dashboard UI Architectures
Massive, clunky dashboard designs were dismantled and replaced with ultra-fast, segmented widget components.
